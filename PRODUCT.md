# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Women and wellness enthusiasts who want a gentle, structured, and aesthetic way to build daily habits, track workouts, plan nutritious meals, and reflect on their glow-up journey without tech overwhelm or clinical pressure.

## Product Purpose

MayaAuraFit provides an aesthetic, clean, pastel printable & digital fitness habit planner (63+ printable pages) that transforms daily consistency into an enjoyable, rewarding ritual. It helps users take the guesswork out of their wellness journey — one page at a time.

## Positioning

Unlike complex, overwhelming bio-tracking tech apps or clinical dashboards, MayaAuraFit is an intentional, tactile, and beautifully crafted pastel wellness planner. It offers calm guidance, habit stacking, hydration tracking, workout logs, meal prep layouts, and reflection prompts designed for real life.

## Operating Context

Used either printed out on paper in home/gym spaces or imported digitally into tablet note-taking apps like GoodNotes and Notability on iPad.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- 63+ printable & digital planner pages
- Instant high-resolution PDF download
- Compatible with print (A4/US Letter) and tablet apps (iPad, GoodNotes, Notability)
- Clean, fast, modern web landing page built in React 19 + Vite + Tailwind CSS v4

## Product Principles

1. **Aesthetic Simplicity:** Clean pastel palette, generous whitespace, and editorial typography that feel calm and inspiring.
2. **Immediate Utility:** Frictionless access to printable habit trackers, meal plans, workout schedules, and progress grids.
3. **No Tech Overwhelm:** Clear, tangible benefits over complex medical or biometric jargon.
4. **Delight in Consistency:** Celebrate small daily wins and self-care routines.
